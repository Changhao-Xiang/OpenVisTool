\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related_work}

\paragraph{Think with Images}
The paradigm of ``think with images'' is first introduced by OpenAI's o3 \citep{openai2025o3}, where visual reasoning is no longer limited to a single static image encoding. Models actively transform, inspect, and verify visual evidence during the reasoning process \citep{su2025thinkingsurvey}. Recent works instantiate this paradigm through a tool-calling agent interface, in which image operations are actions and their outputs become intermediate evidence. They mainly differ in how the tool-use capability is obtained: training-free prompting \citep{hu2024sketchpad,zhao2025pyvision}, reinforcement learning over visual tool use \citep{zheng2026deepeyes,wu2026vtoolr}, and cold-start SFT followed by RL for robust multi-step tool use \citep{su2025openthinkimg,su2026pixelreasoner,hong2026deepeyesv2,zhang2026thyme,lai2026minio3,guo2025codevision,hou2025codev,zhao2026pyvisionrl}. Rather than proposing another training algorithm, \method is an open, cross-domain recipe for synthesizing and filtering visual tool-use trajectories.

\paragraph{Tool-Use Trajectory Synthesis}
Tool-use trajectories are essential for augmenting models with external tools. In text-only tool-integrated reasoning, prior methods synthesize traces for coding, search, or general tool-calling agents \citep{schick2023toolformer,qin2024toolllm,liu2024apigen,chen2023fireact,zeng2024agenttuning}. Multimodal agentic systems extend this idea to visual evidence, distilling trajectories that interleave reasoning, image operations, and returned observations from strong teachers \citep{gao2025multimodal,zhang2026thyme,lai2026minio3,cheng2026openmobilebuildingopenmobile}. In recent trajectory-distillation pipelines, a common recipe is outcome-based rejection sampling: candidate trajectories are generated, and those that are well-formed, executable, and answer-correct are retained. Such filtering ensures that a demonstration reaches the right answer, but it does not establish that the recorded observations were useful, since successful teacher traces may still contain incidental calls. In contrast, \method retains only trajectories that are both answer-correct and tool-beneficial, so the supervision is selected for evidence acquisition rather than tool-call mimicry.
