\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented \method, an open and reproducible recipe for synthesizing
cross-domain visual tool-use trajectories. The recipe spans five complementary
domains under one visual toolset and curates trajectories with double rejection
sampling, pairing answer-correctness checking with a \toolgain criterion that
retains a trajectory only when its tool calls were necessary to reach the
answer. It yields \dataset, an openly released 42K-example agentic SFT corpus.
Fine-tuning on \dataset improves every domain across backbones from 4B to 27B
and on a separate model family, with gains of up to \TODO{+18.9} points
(Visual Search, Qwen3.5-9B); our ablations show that both filters are needed,
as dropping either one degrades the resulting model. We release the dataset and
the full pipeline to support unified, quality-controlled visual tool-use
research.
